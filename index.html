📘 Getting started guide
Install on Android: open this page in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen. It will open full screen like an app.

Keep the timer screen open during a seizure – the alarm sounds from the phone while the page is on screen.

Backup

Save a copy of every entry, or move the log to another phone.

Videos aren't included in backups – save them to the phone from each seizure's details.

Video

Important information

  • Not a medical device. Seizure Tracker is a record-keeping and reminder tool. It has not been assessed, registered or approved by the MHRA or any other regulator.
  • It does not detect, monitor, diagnose or predict seizures. It does not give medical advice either.
  • Always follow the child's care plan. Follow the advice of their doctors and nurses too. If anything in the app differs from the care plan, the care plan wins.
  • In an emergency, call 999. Never wait for the app.
  • Don't rely on it alone. Timers, alarms and reminders depend on the phone – battery, volume, silent mode, the screen being on and signal – and may not sound.
  • Records are made by family and carers. They may be incomplete or inaccurate and are not a clinical record. Check them before sharing.

Using Seizure Tracker means you accept this. Full details are in the getting started guide.

General first-aid guidance (NHS / Epilepsy Action): call 999 if a seizure lasts more than 5 minutes, if one seizure follows another without recovery in between, if the child is injured, or if it's their first seizure. The child's own care plan takes priority – set the alarm to the time their plan gives.

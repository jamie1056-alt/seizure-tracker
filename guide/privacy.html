SEIZURE TRACKER

Privacy notice

How your information, and your child's, is collected, stored and protected. Version 1 · November 2026.

Open the app →

1In short

  • If you use Seizure Tracker on your phone only, nothing you enter leaves your phone.
  • If you sign in, seizure records and care plans are stored by Google in London, UK, and only people the child's lead carer invites can see them.
  • Videos are never uploaded. There's no advertising, no tracking and nothing is sold.
  • You can download your information, or delete it, at any time.

2Who is responsible

Seizure Tracker is a small, non-commercial app run by an individual (Jamie), who is the data controller for information stored in signed-in accounts. It is currently being tested with a small group.

Contact: jamie1056@gmail.com – for questions, requests to see, correct or delete information, or concerns.

If a school, care service or other organisation uses the app for children in its care, that organisation will usually be the controller for its own records and should give you its own privacy information.

3What we collect

InformationExamples
About you (signed-in users)Your name, email address, and your role on each child's team. If you sign in with Google, Google tells us your name and email.

Health information is special category data under UK GDPR, so it gets extra protection.

4Why we use it, and the legal basis

We use the information only to provide the app: to time and record seizures, follow the child's care plan, share the log with the child's team, and produce reports you choose to send.

  • Legal basis: your consent (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(a)), and for health information your explicit consent (Article 9(2)(a)). You give this when you sign in and when you set up a child.
  • A child's information is entered by their parent or guardian, or by someone with their permission.
  • You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting the child's log or asking us to delete your account. This doesn't affect anything done before.

Who can see a child's records: only people the child's lead carer has invited, in the role they were given. This is enforced on Google's servers. The person running the app can technically access stored data to fix problems, but will only do so when needed or when you ask.

Reports you email are sent from your own email account to people you choose.

6How it's protected

  • Encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest.
  • Each person signs in with their own account. Invite links work once and expire after 7 days.
  • Lead carers can remove people instantly and see a history of changes.
  • Signing out removes the shared copy from that phone.

7How long we keep it

  • Records are kept until a lead carer deletes them, or deletes the child's whole log (Settings → People).
  • Your account details are kept while you use the app. Ask us and we will delete them.
  • If an account is unused for 2 years, we may contact you and then delete it.

8Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete or receive a copy of your information, or to restrict or object to its use. Much of this you can do yourself in the app (download a backup, spreadsheet or PDF; edit or delete records; delete a child). Otherwise email jamie1056@gmail.com – we'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how your information is handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or 0303 123 1113.

9Changes to this notice

If this notice changes in a way that matters, the app will show it again and ask you to agree before you continue.
